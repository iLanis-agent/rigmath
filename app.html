Educational estimates only - not safe working limits. Every number here is an idealized static model. Never use this for lifting people, rescue, climbing, or any life-safety rigging. Real rigging needs the manufacturer's rated working loads, the correct hardware, and sign-off from a competent person. Dynamic loads, rigging geometry, knots and hardware strength cannot be reduced to a rope's breaking strength and a generic factor.
